Scientific basis and model boundaries

V3 separates cinematic cell rendering from a weighted biological ledger. The animation communicates ordering, location and movement; the Observatory communicates quantities that cannot be read reliably from sprite counts.

V3 architecture and interpretation

SystemImplementation
Permanent legendActual lineage-specific glyphs remain visible above the timeline. Selecting one or more entries dims unrelated agents without changing the model.
Weighted ledgerCell proportions, titres, affinity, signals and traffic are derived independently of the number of symbols rendered. Mobile and low-density modes therefore report the same biological state.
Tertiary lymphoid structureThe TLS is now an ectopic niche forming inside chronically inflamed peripheral tissue. It remains absent in most acute and vaccine responses instead of occupying an empty permanent organ panel.
Immune ObservatoryComposition, humoral and signal views expose response-associated cell distributions, antibody components, affinity maturation, grouped soluble activities and TLS state.
Accessibility and sharingAll controls have accessible names, charts expose live text summaries, operating-system reduced-motion preferences are respected, touch inspection is supported, and complete model states can be shared by URL or exported as CSV.
Chronic responsePersistent antigen can now produce progressive cytotoxic exhaustion, reducing functional CD8 activity without erasing the expanded population.

Time and movement

Visual processModel basis
Naive lymphocyte surveillanceNaive T cells are rendered faster and more persistently than follicular B cells, consistent with intravital-imaging ranges commonly near 10–18 μm/min for T cells and about 5–7 μm/min for B cells.
Antigen relaySoluble antigen reaches the draining node before most migratory tissue dendritic cells. Free antigen is therefore animated through lymph within hours, while migratory DC arrival is distributed over roughly 12–48 hours.
T-cell primingEarly serial contacts transition into longer contacts and proliferative commitment. Visible expansion begins after the first simulated day and develops over several days.
Germinal centreFollicular expansion begins around days 4–7 in many acute mouse models. Dark- and light-zone cycling, selection, apoptosis and output are represented as weighted agents.
Tertiary lymphoid structureTLS organisation requires persistent local inflammation. It progresses locally from aggregate to follicle-like organisation and may develop a GC-like core only in sustained chronic scenarios.

Interpretation

Values are relative model outputs rather than clinical measurements. One visible agent may represent many biological cells. Biological event time is separated from microscopic movement time. Age uses an illustrative lifespan response curve with reduced early-life competence, stronger young-adult responsiveness and later immunosenescence. Prior immunity remains an aggregate influence, not an individual prediction.
